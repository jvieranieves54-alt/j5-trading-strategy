The 5-Step J5 Execution Protocol

Market Structure + Volume + 20 EMA Quadratic Concavity

Without C4, J5 does not exist. The J5 Protocol takes Creemos Capital's foundation and enhances it with a geometric filter to capture institutional momentum while shielding trades against horizontal consolidation.

Step-by-Step Execution Protocol

Follow all five rules sequentially. Skipping a step invalidates the setup.

1
Identify Key Support or Resistance (Supply & Demand Zones)

All high-probability entries originate strictly from institutional supply or demand zones. Bullish trades are initiated when price reacts off a validated Demand Zone (Support). Bearish trades are executed when price rejects a Supply Zone (Resistance). We never buy directly into resistance or sell directly into support.

Filter: Zone validation requires clear historical rejections on structural timeframes.
2
Trendline & Pullback Execution (5m to 15m Timeframe)

We map trendlines strictly on the 5-minute to 15-minute timeframes. Entry timing is essential: trades are executed exclusively on the 1st or 2nd pullback following a trendline break, targeting a minimum 2:1 risk-to-reward ratio. Later pullbacks carry higher risks of institutional exhaustion.

Rule: Do NOT enter on 3rd or 4th pullbacks due to momentum decay.

3
Pure Price Action & Dow Theory (Zero Oscillators)

We eliminate all lagging indicators such as RSI, Stochastic, or MACD. We rely strictly on Dow Theory: Higher Highs and Higher Lows (HH/HL) for bullish structures, and Lower Lows and Lower Highs (LL/LH) for bearish structures. Price action alone reveals institutional order flow.

4
Volume Confirmation — The Fuel of the Entry

Volume represents true institutional backing. We require a high or moderate volume spike at the moment of entry. Low volume indicates retail participation and increases fakeout probability. Volume acts as the fuel that propels price toward our profit targets.

5
20 EMA Quadratic Concavity & 5% Dynamic Rule

The core mathematical filter of J5: We evaluate the curvature of the 20 EMA. We enter when price separates approximately 5% from the 20 EMA, utilizing it as Dynamic Support in buys or Dynamic Resistance in sells. Curvature must follow quadratic parabolic concavity: $y = -x^2$ for buy momentum and $y = x^2$ for sell momentum.

Iron Rule: NEVER trade counter-trend against the 4-Hour (H4) or Daily (D1) direction. Flat 20 EMA = NO TRADE.
