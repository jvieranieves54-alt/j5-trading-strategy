Prologue — Part I

The Voice of the First Witch

Song Adaptation & Lyrics for Progressive Metal Collaboration

Intro / Spoken Word
I was the first...
The dawn of witchcraft, the primordial flame.
A priestess born of ancient light,
Medea was my name.
Verse I
Beside the Argonauts I stood, a heroine of gold,
To Jason gave my heart and soul, a love of tales retold.
A lover, wife, and mother bound by sacred, solemn vows,
Until the poison of man’s heart broke every vow he swore.
How easily humanity betrays the ones they love—
And burns the grace that descended from above.
Verse II
They forced the mother to a monster, dark and hollow-eyed,
My hands stained red with my own blood, where two young angels died.
The first to wear the mark of shame, the first they sought to hunt,
I fled across a thousand realms to bear their hatred's brunt.
A counselor, a priestess, a lover in the shade,
Yet every crown I ever wore was carved out of a blade.
Bridge
The dark gods witnessed all my pain, my agony, my tears,
They granted me their highest gift to echo through the years.
The curse of immortality, the gift that never dies,
Yet emptiness remained within, beneath eternal skies.
Pre-Chorus
I sought the ancient, hidden paths, the shadows left behind,
To feed the hunger in my soul, the dark depth of my mind.
In sands of black I met the god, Anubis held the key,
He whispered forbidden incantations into me!
Chorus / Climax
Now rise! The first god among gods, enthroned above the stars!
With power like old Kronos, unbound by mortal scars!
Unequaled force, eternal night, the realm begins to sway—
And in this void of power, I feel it drag away...
Direct Narrative Translation (Reference)
"I was the first... The first witch known to the world... I was a priestess, my name is Medea. With the Argonauts, I was a heroine! With Jason, I became a lover, a wife, and a mother... but... Human nature... it is so easy for them to betray those they love. I became a monster, the murderer of my two children... a killer... I was the first to be hunted... I have walked through many realms... as a priestess, as an advisor, and sometimes as a lover... But there was so much pain in my life... The dark gods were my witnesses, and once again I was the first witch to whom the gods granted their blessing... They gave me the gift of eternity... But I feel empty inside... So I recall my days as a witch... I began to search for knowledge and power... Then I met the Egyptian god... Anubis taught me forbidden spells... Once again, I became the first god among gods! Matchless power like that of Kronos... Now I feel..."
