📌 What It Does

This indicator identifies external and internal liquidity zones — the levels where institutional traders place their orders and where retail traders get trapped.

  • 🔵 External Liquidity: Supply/Demand zones — institutional levels (Soportes/Resistencias reales)
  • 🔴 Internal Liquidity: Stop-loss zones — retail traps (donde se queman los traders)
  • 🌍 Session Detection: ASIA / EUROPE / NEW YORK / LOW LIQUIDITY
  • 📊 Volume Filter: Confirms institutional intent
  • 🎯 Entry Signals: BUY / SELL / NO TRADE based on liquidity + structure
